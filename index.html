🎓 THESIS

Automated Detection of Diabetic Retinopathy in Retinal Images Using Deep Learning

Thesis Description

Developed and implemented advanced deep learning architectures, including MobileNetV3 and EfficientNet B0, along with Vision Transformers for automated retinal image analysis.

MobileNetV3 achieved 98.91% accuracy, while EfficientNet B0 achieved 98.1% accuracy.

The study successfully performed binary and multiclass classification tasks to detect and evaluate specific severity stages of Diabetic Retinopathy.

Explainable AI (XAI) techniques were also integrated for visual decision-mapping to improve clinical reliability. Robust preprocessing, data augmentation and hyperparameter optimization were employed throughout the development process.
